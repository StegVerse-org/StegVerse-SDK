# StegVerse Demo Surface Terms of Use

Version: 1.0.0
Effective date: 2026-08-12
Owner: StegVerse-org
Surface: StegVerse Demo / Evaluation SDK Relationship

## 1. Permitted use

A participant may use the Demo Surface only within the capabilities admitted by the current SDK evaluation relationship receipt and subject to applicable component licenses.

Permitted activities may include, when admitted: reading frozen documentation, schemas, examples, manifests, receipts, and source artifacts; running deterministic local demonstrations; evaluating portability, replayability, governance boundaries, performance, interfaces, or other stated objectives; developing against public SDK interfaces; invoking an evaluator-scoped StegGhost sandbox route; invoking an evaluator-scoped LLM-adapter entry point through the SDK; and preparing technical, commercial, integration, research, or development proposals.

The SDK relationship receipt controls the available service interaction surface. It does not replace rights granted by an applicable software license.

## 2. Required conduct

The participant must use only SDK-admitted capabilities; preserve applicable copyright/license notices; identify signer/represented organization accurately; comply with interaction limits, exclusions, frozen-state boundaries, and resource limits; preserve receipt/package integrity when making evaluation claims; and distinguish demo results from production activation, authority, certification, or current live state.

## 3. Prohibited service-surface conduct

The participant may not use the Demo relationship to obtain or attempt to obtain StegVerse credentials, wallet keys, provider secrets, TV/TVC capability material, private-repository credentials, or other non-exposed secret material; bypass SDK restrictions or frozen-state boundaries; convert non-authorizing results into claims of production authority; access active sovereign heartbeat, production wallet controls, custody services, private control-plane state, or production deployment controls unless separately admitted; impersonate another participant; interfere with StegVerse continuity; intentionally introduce destructive or credential-harvesting logic; or represent evaluation access as creating exclusivity, partnership, endorsement, certification, investment, employment, or procurement commitment.

These service-use restrictions do not remove rights independently granted by an applicable open-source license to lawfully obtained software copies.

## 4. LLM-adapter use

LLM-adapter access, when available, must be entered through the SDK evaluation relationship and restricted to the evaluator-scoped adapter capability admitted by that relationship. Direct evaluator access to the adapter's broader sovereign provider, route, custody, credential, heartbeat, production, or internal evidence surfaces is prohibited.

Model output is informational/evaluative output. It is not execution authority, admissibility, standing, governance approval, or a commercial commitment.

## 5. StegGhost use

StegGhost access, when available, must be limited to the evaluator-scoped sandbox route admitted by the SDK relationship. Sandbox access does not create access to production services or internal sovereign state.

## 6. External systems

Third-party accessibility is subordinate to StegVerse continuity and does not become a StegVerse continuity dependency. The participant remains responsible for separately applicable third-party terms when directly using a third-party service.

## 7. Evaluation outputs

Participants may prepare and communicate evaluation findings, proposals, patches, integrations, and other outputs consistent with applicable licenses and any separately executed agreement. A participant must not knowingly present a frozen or bounded demo result as evidence that a different version, production deployment, or unexposed StegVerse capability was evaluated.

## 8. Relationship changes

A participant may narrow requested scope at any time through the SDK. A request to broaden scope is a new request and must be re-evaluated against the current capability catalog, StegVerse policy, terms status, and any required governance boundary. The SDK must fail closed when expansion is not admitted.

## 9. Termination

When a Demo Service relationship is suspended or terminated, service capabilities granted by that relationship cease. Rights under applicable software licenses for copies already lawfully obtained are governed by those licenses, not by the service relationship.

## 10. Acceptance

Use of an SDK-connected Demo Service relationship requires affirmative acceptance of the exact current Demo Terms of Service and Demo Terms of Use versions and hashes. The SDK acceptance receipt must be validated before any relationship result may be issued.
