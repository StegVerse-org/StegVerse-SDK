# ELAN Next-Phase Experiment Procedure

**Goal Task ID:** ELAN-NEXT-PHASE-EXPERIMENT-001  
**COSV ID:** 10100000100000

## Purpose

Observe and preserve the next authentic ELAN source-native output following the previously recorded ELAN non-emission event, then evaluate that output using the evidence criteria frozen before the observation occurs.

## Preconditions

1. Preserve the existing ELAN Run 1 and Run 2 evidence exactly as already recorded.
2. Do not modify, reinterpret, replace, or synthesize any previously recorded ELAN event.
3. Preserve the previously recorded non-emission event as the immediate predecessor event.
4. Freeze the expected evidence fields before receiving or interpreting the next ELAN output.
5. Do not create a synthetic ELAN response for the experiment.

## Frozen Evidence Fields

The following fields must be evaluated after the next authentic ELAN output is received:

1. Event identity and order.
2. Observation boundaries.
3. Exact source-native output.
4. Source-native state exposed by ELAN.
5. Continuity relative to the preceding non-emission event.
6. Exact next emitted transition.
7. Provenance and custody.
8. Immutable manifest reference.
9. Governed-result reference.
10. Deterministic replay result.
11. Reconstruction result.
12. Returned-result evidence.

Any information ELAN does not expose remains `NOT_EXPOSED`.

Any meaning that cannot be established directly from the evidence remains `UNRESOLVED`.

## Experimental Procedure

### Step 1 — Establish the predecessor

Identify the canonical previously recorded ELAN non-emission event.

Verify that its source-native evidence, event identity, ordering, provenance, and associated preserved evidence remain unchanged.

Do not reinterpret the non-emission event.

### Step 2 — Freeze the comparison criteria

Confirm that the twelve evidence fields listed above are fixed before examining the next ELAN output.

No additional evidence field may be added afterward to improve, rescue, or reinterpret the result.

### Step 3 — Obtain the next authentic ELAN output

Wait for the next actual source-native output produced by ELAN.

The experiment begins its new observation only when authentic ELAN output is available.

Absence of a repository update, missing GitHub evidence, elapsed time, or lack of an attached artifact is not itself an ELAN observation.

### Step 4 — Capture the output before interpretation

Preserve the next ELAN output exactly as received.

Record, before semantic interpretation:

- exact output;
- source;
- event identity;
- event ordering;
- available source-native state;
- provenance;
- observation time or source-native timing information, when exposed.

Do not normalize or paraphrase the ELAN output before the exact source-native form is preserved.

### Step 5 — Bind the observation to the experiment

Bind the captured output to:

- the predecessor ELAN evidence;
- the frozen experiment declaration;
- the immutable manifest/reference used for the experiment;
- the experiment correlation identity.

This establishes the evidence chain from the preceding non-emission event to the newly observed output.

### Step 6 — Represent only observed information

Populate the frozen evidence fields using only evidence actually exposed by ELAN or directly established by the preserved evidence chain.

Use:

`NOT_EXPOSED`

for information ELAN does not expose.

Use:

`UNRESOLVED`

for semantic questions that the available evidence does not resolve.

Do not infer hidden intent, consent, refusal, withdrawal, emotion, motivation, or other unexposed internal state.

### Step 7 — Submit the preserved observation through the established governance path

Use the same established experiment-governance path used for the prior ELAN experiment lineage.

Preserve the resulting governed-result reference.

Do not substitute a newly invented governance route merely for this experiment.

### Step 8 — Preserve custody

Record the captured ELAN observation, experiment manifest reference, governed-result reference, and associated evidence under the established custody mechanism.

Verify that the preserved evidence can be identified as belonging to this exact experiment event.

### Step 9 — Perform deterministic replay

Replay the governed experiment input using the preserved exact observation and immutable experiment material.

Record whether the replay result matches the originally preserved governed result.

Do not alter the input to obtain agreement.

### Step 10 — Perform reconstruction

Reconstruct the experiment result from the preserved evidence chain.

Verify that the predecessor event, new ELAN output, manifest, governed result, and custody references reconstruct consistently.

Record any discrepancy exactly rather than resolving it by inference.

### Step 11 — Preserve the returned result

Preserve the result returned through the experiment path exactly as produced.

Associate it with:

- the captured ELAN output;
- governed-result reference;
- custody evidence;
- replay evidence;
- reconstruction evidence.

### Step 12 — Compare against the frozen declaration

Only after Steps 1–11 are complete, compare the observed experiment against the frozen evidence fields.

For each field, record the observed value or one of the required unresolved states:

`NOT_EXPOSED`

`UNRESOLVED`

Do not add post-hoc criteria.

## Completion Condition

The experiment is complete when:

1. the next authentic ELAN source-native output has been captured exactly;
2. its provenance and ordering relative to the preceding non-emission event are preserved;
3. the immutable experiment manifest is bound;
4. the governed result is preserved;
5. custody is preserved;
6. deterministic replay is performed and recorded;
7. reconstruction is performed and recorded;
8. the returned result is preserved; and
9. the complete evidence set has been compared against the frozen declaration without post-hoc modification or semantic inference beyond the available evidence.

## Prohibited Substitutions

The following do not substitute for the next authentic ELAN output:

- repository silence;
- absence of a GitHub commit;
- elapsed time;
- synthetic ELAN messages;
- inferred ELAN behavior;
- StegVerse-generated replacement observations;
- unrelated runtime evidence;
- unrelated evaluator evidence.

The experiment observes ELAN evidence when authentic ELAN evidence exists. It does not manufacture an observation from its absence.
