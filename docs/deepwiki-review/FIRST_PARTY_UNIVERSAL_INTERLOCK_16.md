## Operational boundaries

The universal-entry handoff specifies a staged design and disabled-by-default integrations. The local dispatcher rejects a handler result that attempts to claim authorizing power, execution authority, custody transfer or an admissibility decision. A locally synthesized dispatch receipt is not an original independently witnessed Master Records receipt. The transition-table intake tests use **synthetic** package IDs and commitment candidates; their expected non-authorizing flags describe test fixtures rather than established real-world standing.

The portable interlock transition module validates supplied hash syntax, connection class, predecessor references, governance-mode/profile composition and manifold shape. It expressly does not grant governance authority. A present, syntactically well-formed predecessor reference is not proof that its referenced original receipt exists, is complete or belongs to the claimed participant. The validation of a supplied COMPOSED profile is not evidence of an actual authorized InTr disposition.
