## Publisher and runtime boundary

The [ingress validator](https://github.com/StegVerse-org/StegVerse-SDK/blob/e1dae308f23b126bc84bd3c606c4e378aa73907b/stegverse/manifest_contract.py) verifies structural fields, hashes, the chosen processing capability and applicable candidate/route conditions. The [builder](https://github.com/StegVerse-org/StegVerse-SDK/blob/e1dae308f23b126bc84bd3c606c4e378aa73907b/stegverse/manifest_builder.py) constructs and validates a canonical envelope. The [route resolver](https://github.com/StegVerse-org/StegVerse-SDK/blob/e1dae308f23b126bc84bd3c606c4e378aa73907b/stegverse/route_resolution.py) checks a declared route against the published installed table. The [run-manifest module](https://github.com/StegVerse-org/StegVerse-SDK/blob/e1dae308f23b126bc84bd3c606c4e378aa73907b/stegverse/manifest_execution.py) binds a request and a processor result to local lineage evidence. These are distinct code-level responsibilities: route declarations, a normalized destination profile and a locally hashed result cannot substitute for Interlock/InTr admission, real execution, canonical Master Records custody, authenticated replay or far-side completion.
