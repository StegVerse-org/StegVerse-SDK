## First-party correction for SDK documentation

The installed SDK exposes **separate** manifest construction, ingress validation, published-route resolution and runtime bindings. Some routes are local semantic processors; a manifested route and source-level `execute_manifest` implementation do not by themselves establish an authorized external WorkerCoordinator claim or a completed governed lifecycle. The source records explicitly distinguish local tests, status-only JSON, historical CI and non-authorizing fixture evaluations from authentic operational evidence. Any claim of a completed transition requires the original admitted run's source-bound receipts, exact predecessor continuity and independent Master Records reconstruction.
